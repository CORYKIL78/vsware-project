School management,
made effortless.

MISpal brings students, teachers, parents and school leadership onto one platform — attendance, behaviour, timetabling, enrolment and communication, all in a single sign-in.

Built for every role

One platform, six ways in

Every person in the school gets exactly the tools their role needs — nothing more, nothing hidden.

Leadership

  • School-wide analytics
  • Staff & student oversight
  • Access to data at every level

Admin & Secretary

  • Manage core school data
  • Generate detailed reports
  • Track movement across school

Teachers

  • Record attendance & behaviour
  • Send and receive messages
  • Real-time class dashboard

Timetablers

  • Powerful scheduling tools
  • Live timetable editing
  • Student options generator

Parents & Contacts

  • View records and results
  • Send absence requests
  • Message teachers directly

Students

  • Track your own attendance
  • View live timetable
  • Message teachers and staff
